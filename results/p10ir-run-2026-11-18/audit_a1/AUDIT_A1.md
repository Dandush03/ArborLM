## 4. Meaning (strictly what the reads identify)

(a) EXACT SEARCH HARMS with CR8(T0) 0.006: exact search over the tree's own keys selects slots whose reads are near chance (T2 a_use 0.033; i = 0). CR8 0.006 is below what two random branches would give (2/64 = 0.031; at 8K/4K 0.063 against 0.125), so the tree's nearest keys are not what it reads. Descriptively (auditor's, d1 32K/16K): the slots T0 reads have mean cosine 0.47 to the query against 0.76 for E8, about two thirds of E8 occurrences fall on slots that T0 selects for no trained fact, and only 31% of slots are read by T0 at all. The tree's keys are meaningful only inside the routed branches with the prior; CR8 does not measure what the router misses (ADR §7, §15). The label shows that exact search over these keys harms, not why the tree is below the flat memory, and nothing about the content's quality.
(b) Visit sweep: v = 2 is best and is the trained setting; v = 1 loses about 1.8 bits, and v = 4 and 8 lose 1.0 and 1.4 bits with priors on (0.38 and 0.07 bits with priors off). More candidates do not help at read time, and the prior limits the damage. This does not show that a tree trained with more visits would not do better (ADR §9 limit).
(c) Priors: removing the prior loses 0.94 bits (T1); removing it from the weights only loses 0.38 (T1w), so about 0.56 bits is selection. 92% of facts span both branches. The prior is load-bearing in the trained read (g_P −0.42); the ablations do not show that the prior causes the gap.
(d) Index: quality follows recovered attention mass almost one-to-one (32K/16K run: AM_C 0.43/0.57/0.72/0.86/0.95 against a_use 0.42/0.58/0.77/0.91/0.98), and a_use is slightly above CR8 because the heaviest slots are found first. A post-hoc k-means index of 16-wide flat keys finds 69% of the exact top 8 at 512 candidates (I256@512: 0.52) and needs about N/8 candidates at 32K/16K and N/4–N/2 at 8K/4K to be RESTORED. This is a recall loss by construction (ADR §7). It says nothing about a trained index.
(e) Reads more favourably or more strongly than the records support: (1) the summary block prints the run-length tree component without TREE STILL LEARNING, which holds 5/5 at both primary cells (the tag appears only in the cell sections); (2) the row is printed as "EXACT SEARCH HARMS" without the ADR's wording "— NOT SEPARABLE AT READ TIME" and without its limiting claim (`labels.py:49` defines EXACT_SEARCH_LONG; the report never uses it); (3) the testability note "(near 1: … not testable)" is printed even where the share is 0.064 (32K/16K run), where CANDIDATES was testable and T3 was worse. Reads less favourably: TREE STILL LEARNING is also attached to the index label (`report.py:102`), although the flat memory passes the plateau check 5/5. "ONLY ABOVE 512" at 8K/4K U_P means a median c* of half the store. Cosmetic: budgets are printed in string order.
(f) Rulings 1–11 changed no label against the ADR's literal text: no CELL-DEPENDENT value (1), no MIXED-PANEL replicate at a primary cell (2), ruling 3 is printed only, rulings 4/9 concern A2 and R-CONFIRM (NOT RUN here), 5/6/8/10 are process (gates passed), and 7/11 had no item error. My literal reading of P/C/J ((RESTORED on both panels) or (closure ≥ 0.5 on both)) equals the code's per-panel OR here: RESTORED with a gap of at least 0.5 implies closure of at least 0.5.
