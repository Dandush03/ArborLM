### ADVISORY

- **A1.** Charged hours are the ledger's wall-clock hours (P10-S0: `train-q1` 13,415 s charged against a task sum of 79,904 s / 6 = 13,317; `train-probe` 5,999 s against a single task of 6,000 s). Sum/6 is a good estimate only when the tasks are many. Stage R rounds and an extension round of four runs are not (one 8K/16K extension run is about 5.4 h of wall time against 3.5 h of sum/6). Add to §5.1 item 2: "P is the summed worker-seconds divided by six, plus the longest single task of each stage (a bound on the makespan)."
- **A2.** §5.1 item 2: say that candidates not yet decided enter P at T = 8,192 exposures (their status if refused).
