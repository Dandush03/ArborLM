- **R-62. Which words are names (audit C2 and its notes).** The decided reading is (i): names relabelled only, exact duplicates of the rendered question removed. (a) "orange" in `M1.shopping_r3` is one of that lineage's 24 object words and is relabelled like the others (the stricter correction; made before any draw of record). (b) The key also relabels object words, event words and invented category words, as well as personal and place names. That is wider than personal names and makes fewer items distinct, which is less favourable to passing; the lead keeps it: in these templates those words are interchangeable labels that the solution procedure does not read. (c) `R2.caesar_shift` keeps its invented words: there they are the text being shifted, and relabelling them changes the answer (1,400 of 1,400 sampled items, the auditor). The exception is right under reading (i) and it does favour the lineage against a mechanical relabelling; it was written with the partition possibly in view (R-61) and is reported to the human lead for his confirmation with the capacity results. The lineage is in part B of R2.
