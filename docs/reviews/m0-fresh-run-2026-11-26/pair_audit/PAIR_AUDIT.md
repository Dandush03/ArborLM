## 3. Solver quality, by reading
Read in full: `solver2_r1.py`, `solver2_r2a.py`, `solver2_r2b.py`, `solver2_m1a.py` (solvers), `solver2_m1b.py`, `solver2_m1c.py`; `solver2_c1.py` lines 1-845 except the text of some error branches. Every solver computes from `structured` by the procedure the question states. No table keyed by item content, no lineage- or level-specific constants, no special case that looks fitted. Notes:
- r1: knights_knaves enumerates all assignments and requires a unique kind for the asked person; venn uses full Moebius inversion over up to four groups; categories takes the first group of the specific-to-general chain with a rule on the verb ("the smaller group wins"); exchange and weekday propagate both ways and raise on inconsistency. Outside the range: `grid_walk` raises for any convention other than the one seen; `compass` raises on turns that are not multiples of 90.
- r2a: binary search follows the stated 1-based floor midpoint; CRT is general (non-coprime moduli handled, "smallest positive" maps 0 to the modulus); `dictionary_rank` by factorial-number-system arithmetic. Outside the range: `expression_eval` rejects unary minus; `caesar_shift` accepts only lowercase and spaces.
- r2b: heap insert and remove exactly as the text describes; remove raises on equal children (the text does not say which is chosen; no item has the tie). `to_base_wide` handles bases 2-16 only (the text's digits).
- m1a: closed forms checked for integrality and range (ages, consecutive, average_missing); `crt_congruence` searches one period by brute force (slow for large moduli); `finite_differences` checks every given term lies on the stated degree and that the degree is exact; `clock_duration` raises on legs that cross midnight or overlap.
- m1b: `last_digit_power` uses the `structured` `op`; its author notes that single-term items have `op = "sum"` while the text says "the product of N factors" (same value), a small structured/text inconsistency in the item, not in the solver. The `fmt_dec2` half-away-from-zero rounding never runs (all values exact to the cent).
- m1c: quadratic shapes solved by exact integer square root with root checks; tiered `units` mode by monotone search; `tip_split` rounds half up only if a value is not whole cents (never happens).
- c1: a general interpreter for the Python subset (scoping with local-name analysis, aliasing, `list +=` in place, floor division and modulo from magnitudes, CPython slice adjustment, `str`/`repr` rendering, a 5,000,000-step cap). It raises `Unsupported` for anything else (other methods, `for` over non-range, keyword args), so programs outside the grammar fail loudly rather than silently.
