# M0 lineage repair: lead rulings for the narrow round (2026-11-25)

By the scientific lead (Claude Fable 5.2), under the human lead's fifth decision (`HUMAN_LEAD_WORDS_5.md`). R-23 (stop) is lifted for the work below and nothing else.

- **R-24 (the bound).** Shortcut bound 0.15, two-sided, set by the human lead. A lineage is dropped if, at any registered level, any cheap rule in the union of the implementer's battery and the auditors' rule scripts (audit3 and audit4 for that evaluation) scores more than 0.15 above or below the base rate, measured as those scripts measure it. A cluster survives if at least one of its lineages survives.
- **R-25 (allowed repairs).** Only: (a) the package-hygiene failures; (b) wording defects named in audit 4 (ambiguous or not uniquely answerable items), repaired by changing the wording or the stated convention, never by rejecting items on their answer; (c) collapsed levels, repaired so that every level produces distinct items with difficulty increasing by a stated parameter. Any lineage with another audit-4 defect (a cheap rule above the bound, answer-conditional rejection not needed for well-posedness, a closed form, a capacity failure that widening would not cure without collapsing levels) is dropped. No new lineage, no replacement shape.
- **R-26 (no loop).** After the repairs the auditors' rule scripts are run again on every surviving lineage. Anything above the bound, or with levels still not distinct, or failing capacity, is dropped, not repaired. There is no further repair round.
- **R-27 (in-place edits).** The hygiene repairs edit the fix-3 files in place: the frozen hygiene test scans the whole package, the files are unregistered work on an unpushed branch, and no lineage hash has been registered. Each edit is listed in the implementer's notes. Wording and level repairs go in new files (`*_fix4*`), with new manifests.
- **R-28 (pool builder).** Capacity is computed against `core.generate_pool` as written (R-22). A lineage that passes only under a per-cluster builder is reported as such and its fate is left to the addendum; it is not counted as passing.
- **R-29 (words).** The private sets are described as descriptive readings wherever a margin reads NOT EVALUABLE. Nothing in this round is a registration.
