LEAD RULING: strict; the general algorithm is the procedure; number system, constraint mix, final question, names, order,

Ambiguous sentence: "a distinct solution procedure" -- at what granularity is "procedure" read (the named method a solver executes
after translating the words, vs. the hand-written template's own setup). STRICT below = merge whenever the two lineages reduce, after
translation (surface frame), to one general algorithm whose remaining differences are only the lead's listed parameters
(final question, constraint mix, number system). LENIENT = merge only when one lineage is a mechanical variant of the other on the
ADR's own list (parameters, order, negation, names, surface frame), with the final question and the core computation unchanged.

STRICT CLUSTERS (merges; reasons)
S1 linear-solve {linear_equation, ages, consecutive, average_missing, pipes_work, rectangle, coins}
   reason: after translation each is "isolate the unknown in a linear relation" (coins and ages: substitute one unknown, then the same);
   differences are surface frame, constraint mix (1 vs 2 equations) and the final question (rectangle reports k w^2, consecutive the largest term).
S2 weighted-sum {shopping, speed_distance, mixture}
   reason: sum of products w_i x_i; mixture and speed_distance's average variant divide by sum w_i (identical procedure);
   shopping subtracts the sum from the amount paid (final question).
S3 multiplicative-scaling {percent_change, fraction_remainder, tip_split, ratio_share}
   reason: multiply a stated amount by a chain of rational factors (1 +- p/100; 1 - 1/a; 1/n; p_i / sum p); percent vs unit fraction is number system.
   ratio_share is the weakest member (needs the sum of the parts first) -- AMBIGUOUS, strict-only.
S4 counting-by-product-rule {binomial_choose, slot_permutations}       AMBIGUOUS, strict-only
   reason: both count selections by the multiplication principle; unordered vs ordered (divide by k!) and the restrictions are constraint mix.
S5 unbounded-knapsack table {change_ways, min_cost_packs}             AMBIGUOUS, strict-only
   reason: both are the same DP over amounts with unbounded item supply; "count the combinations" vs "minimise cost of at least N" is the final question.
S6 exposed-faces-of-unit-cells {grid_perimeter, painted_cube}          AMBIGUOUS, strict-only
   reason: a literal solver loop "for each unit cell, count its faces not shared with another cell of the shape" answers both; 2-D vs 3-D and
   "total exposed edges" vs "cells with exactly f exposed faces" are parameter / final question.
S7 arithmetic accumulation {arithmetic_series, recurrence_iterate}     AMBIGUOUS, strict-only (cross existing/new)
   reason: arithmetic_series is "iterate t_{i+1} = t_i + d and accumulate"; recurrence_iterate's add_index shape is exactly a sum of an
   arithmetic progression (t_n = a + 2 + 3 + ... + n); both are "apply the stated step rule n times".
All other lineages are singletons under strict.

LENIENT MERGES

   each applied to the price before it" -- a mechanical variant (number system + surface frame) of the same base rule.
No other merge under lenient.

NEAR-MISSES KEPT APART (both readings)
- median_list / mode_tally: sort-and-select vs tally-and-argmax; different algorithms (the author's borderline pair; I keep it apart).
- change_ways / digit_puzzle: counting all solutions of a linear Diophantine equation vs filtering 900 candidates by digit clues; different.
- last_digit_power / crt_congruence: exponent cycle mod 10 vs solving simultaneous congruences; different.
- gcd_lcm / prime_factors: gcd/lcm can be done by factorising, but Euclid / listing multiples is the canonical procedure; kept apart (closest strict near-miss in number theory).
- gcd_lcm (lcm) / crt_congruence: both can be sieved over multiples; CRT needs residue matching; kept apart.
- recurrence_iterate / finite_differences: finite differences must INFER the rule (difference table) before iterating; kept apart.
- quadratic_area / pythagoras: quadratic root vs square root of a sum of squares; the quadratic "squares" shape resembles Pythagoras but solves a quadratic; kept apart.
- quadratic_area / rectangle (E): quadratic vs linear; kept apart.
- tiered_pricing / S2 weighted-sum: tiered needs the boundary split (min(left, size)) and has an inverse mode; kept apart.
- coins (E) / change_ways: unique solution of a 2x2 system vs counting all solutions; kept apart.
- division_counting / pipes_work: ceiling/floor division vs exact division; kept apart.
- pigeonhole_worst_case / division_counting: worst-case adversary sum vs ceilings; kept apart.
- inclusion_exclusion / anything in M1: none close.
- clock_duration / S2 weighted-sum: base-60 subtraction and summation; kept apart (strict reader could argue "evaluate a stated expression", which would collapse all arithmetic lineages; I do not read the ruling that far).
- tip_split / ratio_share under lenient: kept apart.
- arithmetic_series / consecutive under lenient: forward AP sum vs inverse; kept apart (strict puts consecutive in S1).
