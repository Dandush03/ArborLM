## Not applied verbatim, or chosen by the lead

1. **E1, the 0.50 bracket.** The auditor's bracket "[0.50 is the lead's draft; … 0.70 …; the lead fixes one before the freeze]" is replaced by 0.50, the number the human lead agreed; 0.70 is recorded as the alternative not taken (ruling A).
2. **E1, the DOES NOT COMPOSE clause.** The auditor wrote "if, in some order, every replicate has H_a < 0.50 for some answer", which can be read as a different answer in each replicate. The final text says "for some order and some answer a, H_a < 0.50 in every replicate of that order": the same answer in every replicate, which is what the human lead agreed ("some answer keeps less than half its gain in every replicate of an order"). This is the narrower reading, so DOES NOT COMPOSE prints less often and COMPOSITION INCONCLUSIVE more often; it does not affect COMPOSES.
3. **E1, C1.** The auditor's sentence "In C1, H_German = 1 by construction, so C1 tests the French answer in context" is replaced, because ruling C alternates the order. Added to the definition of s_a: "over the items in which a is tested (C2: all items; C1: the items where a is the second answer)". "The same sentences" for the single-task, Stage 0 and teacher scores therefore means, in C1, the sentences on which that answer stands second (about n/2).
4. **E1, B markers.** The two tightenings are marked inside the row as B(i) and B(ii). They were first marked pending; the human lead then confirmed them ("I agree with your recommendation", recorded in `HUMAN_DECISIONS_2026-11-15.md`), so every pending mark and the fallback wording are removed.
5. **E3, last sentence** ("Add to the L-sep row: printed with the caveat of §7 (E8)"). Not applied: the caveat belongs to E8 option (ii), and ruling D adopts option (i).
6. **E5 item 10, "the ensemble prints H = 1".** Qualified to "H = 1 when Stage 0's combined score does not exceed its single-task score, and never more than 1", which is what the max floor of B(ii) gives.
7. **E7.** §12 rows 12–13 are rewritten around the strengthened rule rather than pasted, because the row for the denominator and the row for the 0.50 cut are now separate entries.
8. **m7.** The auditor's condition "|R_A(s1)| = 1 and |R_B(s2)| = 1" is kept for German-first items and generalized to the reference sets actually used in French-first items.
9. **m5.** The formula is written with T1 and T2 instead of A and B.
10. **Ruling C, consequences the ruling did not spell out (the lead's):** the single-task answers are defined per sentence and language, taken from a C1 first answer where one exists and otherwise from a separate pass of n answers (same count as the draft's S_B^C pass); n odd is handled by the same rule; the rehearsal's cyclic pairing alternates by parity too; the ensemble test (§11 item 7) is changed because C1 no longer covers both languages of every sentence.
11. **Ruling D, cost.** The increment for L-sep's recomputed prefix (at most 500 single answers per replicate at the slow end) is the lead's estimate and arithmetic, not checked by the auditor, who called the cost negligible.
12. **E1's instruction for the ADR header and §18** ("add one line recording the human lead's decision verbatim") is listed in §14 as an edit to make at adoption; the ADR itself was not edited.
