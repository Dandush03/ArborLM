## Question and exclusions

P1 failed promotion and P2 did not meet its registered dependence/sensitivity criteria. Those outcomes remain unchanged under every possible P3 result. This tests insufficient training duration on a fixed tiny dataset, not the need for more unique data, architecture redesign, novelty, reasoning, generalization, SSD viability or a competitive compute frontier.

Continue every P2 cell, without selecting favorable seeds: B=101/102/103, M=201/202/203, O=301/302/303 (27 learned), and the 9 B/O controls with M=201 memory disabled throughout. Same order as P2: ascending B, then O, control first, then M ascending. There is no additional model initialization or optimizer restart. The three measured durations are repeated observations on each same trajectory, not independent training replicas.

## Prespecified analysis and bounded conclusions

At each duration t define D_t=disabled-enabled, P_t=shuffled-enabled, G_t=paired control-enabled, S_t=paired control-disabled; verify G_t=D_t+S_t to 1e-12. All terms are from the same P3 panel and matching duration. Report all raw NLLs and D/P/G/S for all cells/durations, mean/sample SD/min/max/sign counts, control distributions using 9 unique controls, and trajectories. Report each learned model's and control's NLL change from 256 to 512 and 1024.

Primary duration effects are paired **ΔD=D_1024-D_256** and **ΔP=P_1024-P_256** for all 27 learned cells. Report corresponding step-512 changes as secondary, with no selection or additional success criterion. Separately report ΔG and ΔS and both 256/1024 absolute values; increases in intervention sensitivity can arise from coadaptation or degradation and do not alone demonstrate better prediction.

Descriptive flags fixed before outcomes (screening sensitivities, not significance tests):

1. **Duration increases dependence at registered sensitivity:** mean ΔD >=0.005 AND mean ΔP >=0.005 AND at least 22/27 cells have BOTH ΔD>0 and ΔP>0.
2. **Endpoint dependence at registered sensitivity:** mean D_1024 >=0.005 AND mean P_1024 >=0.005 AND at least 22/27 cells have BOTH D_1024>0 and P_1024>0.
3. **Endpoint quality guard:** mean enabled NLL at 1024 is strictly below its mean at 256 AND mean G_1024 >=0. Both must hold. Report how many individual trajectories improve/worsen, even if the mean improves.

Only if all three hold, record bounded support that this fixed-budget continuation relieved weak useful memory dependence on this panel. Otherwise state exactly which conditions failed; preserve positive individual trajectories and negative/null aggregate outcomes. No outcome reverses P1/P2, proves universal undertraining or licenses scaling/redesign automatically. Even if all hold, more examples, independent corpora, fair compute/tuning baselines and replication remain separate questions. Do not infer significance from this correlated grid, shared controls/data or three repeated durations. No p-values or independent-replicate confidence intervals. Report duration interactions by B/M/O through marginal means/ranges and additive-residual interaction RMS for ΔD/ΔG using the same descriptive formula as P2; no extra factor threshold or selected-seed follow-up.

### Pre-run evaluation-count clarification

The reference evaluation and fresh enabled replay are distinct. Per duration, learned cells perform five new-panel evaluations: unchanged reference, fresh enabled replay, disabled, values-permuted and joint permutation. Controls perform two: unchanged reference and fresh replay, both with memory bypassed. Thus a complete suite has 459 new-panel evaluations (27*3*5 + 9*3*2), plus the 36 separately labeled old-panel parent-integrity evaluations. There is no second redundant enabled replay. This clarification was recorded during implementation, before any P3 model evaluation; it changes no scientific condition, metric or threshold.
