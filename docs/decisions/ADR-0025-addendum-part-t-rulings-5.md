# ADR-0025 addendum: Part T rulings 28 to 30 (fifth addendum)

Status: rulings of the lead coordinator after the fourth independent audit of 938d733 (N9, N10 and ruling 27 closed; C1-C3 to fix before calibration). They bind like the ADR and the earlier addenda (none is edited). Recorded by the implementer from the coordinator's message; not a human-lead approval record beyond what the coordinator states. `Astra review: NOT_PERFORMED`.

28. **Code identity between calibration sessions (C1).** Each session's start line carries the hashes of the computing files (every `studies/p10is2/*.py`) and of the config. A resume refuses when they differ from the first session's: that is the case of the single recorded repetition (ruling 26), never a silent continue. The final record's hashes must equal every session's.
29. **No unseen redraw (C2; favours the memory).** Calibration runs in two steps. `calibrate --open` requires the tracked log to be clean and equal to HEAD, appends the session's start line (with ruling 28's hashes) and exits without running any unit; the lead commits it. `calibrate` (run) refuses unless HEAD's log carries an open start line for the repetition that the working file extends, and unless the calibration directory's state is consistent with the log (a directory that does not hold the session HEAD's log opened, or state that is missing under a log that lists finished units, is refused). A resume after a stop needs no new commit: the INTERRUPTED line is appended and the same open session continues; the lead commits the end line before the plan (the plan already reads HEAD).
30. **The counting rehearsal's cap (C3).** The counting rehearsal's cap is its own plan allowance (rulings 20 and 27), not the fixed 3.0 h rehearsal cap; the measuring rehearsal keeps the fixed cap. If the allowance makes the plan's total exceed the study's budget bound (`caps.study_budget_hours`, set by the human lead; 0.0 means not set), the candidate plan and the registered caps refuse and it goes to the human lead: no automatic extension.
