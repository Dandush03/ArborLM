# ADR-0025 addendum — rulings for the implementation of Part T (2026-11-20)

Rulings of the scientific lead (Claude Fable 5.2) on the readings that the first implementation audit of Part T left open (`docs/reviews/p10-i-s2-partt-audit-2026-11-20/AUDIT_P10IS2_PARTT.md`, arbor-auditor subagent, Claude Opus 5.5; verdict NOT READY: the scientific core matches an independent reference on 60,000 synthetic registries, the engineering around it does not meet the pattern). Made before any Part T value exists: no Part T rehearsal, calibration or registered stage has run. They change no threshold and no label rule. Where a reading could move a label, the one less favourable to the memory is taken. They are reported to the human lead, who can overrule any of them before Part T's Decision 2. ADR-0025 is not edited. `Astra review: NOT_PERFORMED`. Claude review is not Astra review.

1. **(Q1, audit B2) Rehearsals never use the registered training draws and never score an evaluation panel.** A Part T rehearsal trains on a rehearsal draw of its own from the accepted pool, made with a rehearsal-range seed that the seed registry allocates for that purpose (to be added by a registry revision before the rehearsal; no registered seed is reused), and scores D_cal only (and D_con where the M-ii constraint needs it). Gate G2 in a rehearsal scores D_cal. E_A, E_B, E_C and E_open are never scored before the registered run of Part T (section 4.1), and the code refuses to score them outside a registered stage. Snapshots trained in a rehearsal are kept in the rehearsal directory and can never be read by a registered stage.
2. **(Q2) Calibration draws may overlap the registered draws, and this is disclosed.** They cannot be made disjoint: teacher A's accepted pool is 24,187 items and the three registered replicates take 24,000 disjoint items, leaving 187. A calibration draw is training material only; calibration scores D_cal only and fixes U and the rates by code. The overlap is stated in Decision 2 and in the outcome record. No evaluation item is involved: the pools exclude every evaluation reference by construction (Part Q dropped nine outputs per teacher for that reason).
3. **(Q3) The Q_fit panel is used by Part T for one thing only:** the disjointness check of gate G1, by keys and references, with no score read. It is excluded from every training set, panel, bank and report.
4. **(Q4) Part T's charge before the freeze** is every Part T process up to the moment the caps are computed: the measuring rehearsal, the calibration and any repetition of it, and any earlier counting rehearsal, each charged to one Part T ledger as it runs. The final counting rehearsal's own charge does not exist when the caps are set and is outside the total, as in Part Q's attempt 3; its seconds are recorded in Decision 2. Part Q's 13.48 charged hours belong to Part Q and are not counted again. A stage cap of 0 is never read as "uncapped": every registered stage, the recomputation included, has a cap by the rule or the freeze refuses.
5. **(Q5) AGAINST-LOOKUP names the baseline that gave b for each cell and capability,** not the most frequent one.
6. **(Q6) Gate G1 takes the Q_check groups and keys from Part Q's partition records,** read by SHA-256 from the Part Q run of record (attempt 3, whose stages 1 to 6 are attempt 2's, reused).
7. **(Q7, audit B3) The recomputation is a registered stage with its own cap, placed before the analysis,** as section 10 lists it. The evaluation results are merged into the registry at the end of the stages that produce them, so that no stage needs a record that only it or a later stage writes. No result is reported before the recomputation has passed (section 8.6).
8. **(Q8) Routers are snapshotted with their hashes and re-read by the recomputation,** and the recomputation states that it read them. A router is never retrained for the recomputation.
9. **(Q9, audit R13) In a CONSOLIDATION-REJECTED cell the pinned M-append snapshot is what every later reading uses, CONTROL-FAILED included.** The unpublished M-ii state enters no label.
10. **(audit R1, R2) Part T reads Part Q through one checked door.** The Part Q run directory of record is named in the Part T plan; every Part Q file Part T reads is listed there with its SHA-256 (from Part Q's manifest, or from the reuse hashes for the files of the six reused stages) and re-hashed before use; a difference is PAUSED — IDENTITY.
11. **(audit R5) Everything the independent recomputation needs is stored:** per-item scores and greedy sequences of every registered score, and the snapshots it reads. Part Q stored only two means for the untrained student (its outcome record, correction 1); Part T stores the per-item values of every quantity a gate or a label reads.
12. **(audit R6) The controls stage does not repeat the evaluation pass;** it scores the controls of section 5.2 on their registered subsamples only.
13. **(audit R7, R8) No record is overwritten.** A calibration that is run again writes a new record and the reason for the repetition; the plan takes U, the rates and the pilot result from the calibration's own record by hash, never from a hand-assembled file. A failed recomputation record is kept and a rerun writes beside it.
14. **(audit R9) ENGINEERING-FAILURE is reachable** by every cause section 8.6 names, and the ensemble integrity check it names is implemented.
15. **The timing benchmark (stage 7)** keeps its registered quiet rule (no study process alive; one-minute load at most 2.0 for 60 s before each repetition; NOT MEASURED after five failed attempts by the lead's recorded decision). On this machine the idle load measured on 2026-11-19 had a 95th percentile of 1.74 with the application closed, so 2.0 is expected to be reachable; that is an expectation from one evening's log, not a measurement of this stage. No label reads the benchmark. Its process rule must count the stage's own launcher as family (audit R11).
