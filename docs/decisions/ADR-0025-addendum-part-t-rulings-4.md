# ADR-0025 addendum: Part T rulings 24 to 27 (fourth addendum)

Status: rulings of the lead coordinator after the third independent audit of c344251 (N1-N8 and R14 closed; four items to fix before the measuring rehearsal). They bind like the ADR and the earlier addenda (none is edited). Recorded by the implementer from the coordinator's message; not a human-lead approval record beyond what the coordinator states. `Astra review: NOT_PERFORMED`.

24. **The rehearsal draw and G1 (audit N9).** In a rehearsal D_cal stands in for the evaluation panels, so the rehearsal training draw is cleaned by the rule Part Q applied to evaluation references: every item whose output (normalized) equals a normalized reference of a stand-in panel is dropped from the draw; the number dropped per capability is recorded in the rehearsal record. G1 then runs unchanged and must pass. G1 is not weakened and not turned into a report-only check in any mode.
25. **A stopped calibration is PAUSED (audit N10; ADR section 7.1 rule 4).** A calibration session that stops (cap, out of memory, power loss, non-finite value, kill) is resumable by the same command. On restart a `start` without an `end` gets an appended `INTERRUPTED` line with the reason found (or UNKNOWN), and the session continues from its stored per-unit state without redrawing anything already drawn. A non-finite value is recorded as that unit's outcome under the ADR's own rule and is not retried. No hand edit of the log is ever needed. Each resume gets what is left of the calibration cap.
26. **The calibration log is committed (audit N2 residual; favours the memory).** The candidate plan and the freeze read the log from git HEAD, never the working file; they refuse if the working file differs from HEAD, if the log's history in git is not append-only (every earlier committed version a prefix of the next), or if the calibration ledger's sessions do not match the logged ones. `second/` is used only when a committed lead record `results/p10is2-partt-calibration/REPETITION.md` names the reason and the commit of the code change that forced it; a code edit alone never switches the record used.
27. **The counting rehearsal at full U (ADR-0025 section 11 item 5).** The counting rehearsal runs one cell of each trained type at the calibrated full U (so the 500-update checkpoint and stability at full U are rehearsed); the measuring rehearsal stays at 200 updates. The counting allowance of ruling 20 is projected accordingly: the measuring record's training units scaled by the calibrated U over the measuring rehearsal's updates, at the measured rates. The calibration cap's D_cal scoring is counted at D_cal's real size, `data.d_cal_panel = 500`.
