# ADR-0025 addendum: Part T rulings 21 to 23 (third addendum)

Status: rulings of the lead coordinator after the second independent audit of b5f9f7c (`READY AFTER LISTED FIXES`, all to land before calibration). They bind like the ADR and the earlier addenda (neither is edited). Recorded by the implementer from the coordinator's message; not a human-lead approval record beyond what the coordinator states. `Astra review: NOT_PERFORMED`.

21. **One calibration (audit N2, which favours the memory).** The calibration lives in ONE fixed directory derived from the repository's experiments directory (`P10IS2-T-calibration` beside Part Q's run directories; no free path on the real path). Every calibration session appends a line to a tracked log under `results/` (`results/p10is2-partt-calibration/CALIBRATION_LOG.jsonl`). The candidate plan takes only that directory's record, pinned by SHA-256; the freeze refuses another record, a missing directory, or a session the log does not list. The single permitted repetition of ADR-0025 section 7.1 rule 4 is a recorded `second/` with its reason; the first is kept. `calibration.repetition_used` is the rule that picks the record and is called.
22. **Order and rehearsals (audit N4, ADR-0025 sections 7.1 and 10 as written).** The rehearsals run at the ADR's 200 updates, not at the calibrated U, so the measuring rehearsal needs no calibration result. Order: measuring rehearsal, then the caps (a cap for calibration from the same projection, charged + 2 x projected), then calibration under that cap, then the candidate plan, the counting rehearsal and the freeze. The registered stage caps need U and are written after calibration, from the same record. Both rehearsals can resume. Controls, recompute, analysis and report each get a cap and the memory guard. Rehearsal registries and any label-worded rehearsal report are written under a sealed directory.
23. **The A4 lines (R14).** The auditor reads sections 8.5 and 5.3 as requiring the seen-item scores, the E_open scores, the edit-distance-1 share and the scores without those items. They are implemented with the reads they need, through the checked door. They are reported readings and enter no label.
