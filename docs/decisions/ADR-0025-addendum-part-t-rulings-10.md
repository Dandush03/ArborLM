## Ruling 41

**`caps.study_budget_hours = 95.68`** (configs/p10is2_partt.toml).

- **Who and why.** The lead coordinator decided it, under the human lead's delegation above, because ruling 33 makes the bound the human lead's to set and it is the only thing blocking calibration while he is away until Monday 2026-11-23. **He may overrule it.**
- **The rule.** The upper cost figure the human lead was shown (ADR-0025: "The whole study is about 25–110 charged hours"), minus everything Part Q has charged: 13 ledgers, 51,518.98 s = 14.311 h (P10IS2-Q-registered 13.09 s; registered2 26,803.98; registered3 1,039.64; rehearsal-counting2 3,164.12; counting3 56.88; counting4 919.02; counting5 4,857.19; counting6 2,022.07; counting7 963.28; rehearsal-measure 959.32; measure2 3,468.38; measure3 3,229.70; measure4 4,022.32; `P10IS2-Q-rehearsal-counting-20261118` has no ledger; the total equals the 14.31 h the Part Q outcome record gives "had they counted"). So 110 − 14.311 = 95.69, set to **95.68** (the subtraction stays conservative). The same precedent as P10-I-S0's B = 55.
- **The consequence (the auditor's computation, eighth audit).** Ruling 30 checks the plan's total cap, which doubles the projected stages and adds the counting allowance, against this bound; the bound is on a padded cap, the ADR's 110 h on charged hours. From the measured rates of the third measuring rehearsal the total cap is about 71–81 h at a calibrated U = 1,000, 82–92 h at 2,000, 105–115 h at 4,000 and 150–160 h at 8,000. So at a calibrated U of 4,000 or more the candidate plan REFUSES and waits for the human lead; **the lead will not raise the bound.**
- **The plan charge.** All three Part T measuring ledgers enter the plan charge: attempt 3's through the pinned measuring record by rule, attempts 1 (`experiments/P10IS2-T-measuring-20261120`, 1,613.16 s) and 2 (`experiments/P10IS2-T-measuring2-20261120`, 10,800.64 s) through `--charged-ledger`, together with the calibration ledger.
- **What was read.** No rate, score or calibration value was read to choose the bound beyond the cost record (the ADR's figure and the Part Q ledgers).
