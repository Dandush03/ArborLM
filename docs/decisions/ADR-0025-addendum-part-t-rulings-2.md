# ADR-0025 addendum: Part T rulings 16 to 19 (second addendum)

Status: rulings of the lead coordinator on the implementer's open points after the B1/B4 pass (commit 94d9a22). They bind like the ADR and the first rulings addendum (`ADR-0025-addendum-part-t-rulings.md`, not edited). 16. **The rehearsal world.** Four disjoint parts of D_cal standing in for E_A, E_B and E_C (the fourth stays D_cal) are accepted. No evaluation item may be read in any rehearsal. A test asserts that the rehearsal bundle's item ids are disjoint from every E set (E_A, E_B, E_C, E_open) and from Q_check; the code refuses a rehearsal bundle that is not.
17. **The counting rehearsal's own charge.** The freeze reads the counting rehearsal's ledger, records the charge, and adds it to the pre-freeze charge from which the registered ledger starts. The freeze refuses if plan charge + counting charge + 2 x projected exceeds the total cap. No cap is recomputed after counting. (Implementer's note, PENDING LEAD REVIEW: the total cap is computed as plan charge + 2 x projected rounded up to 0.01 h, so this check refuses unless that rounding slack covers the counting charge. If the counting rehearsal is to be affordable, its allowance has to enter the plan charge before the caps are computed. The check is implemented as ruled.)
18. **GEN seconds** are folded into the `score:plain` rate: accepted as recorded (listed separately in each scoring task's record).
19. **Ruling 6 departure** (G1 tests Q_check by English key only, because Part Q stored no Q_check identity groups): stands as a disclosed limitation, pending the auditor's reading of whether any Part T label reads Q_check. The report template states it.
20. **The counting allowance (replaces the check of ruling 17; the rest of ruling 17 stands).** Ruling 17's check was the coordinator's error: with the total cap computed before counting it could not be satisfied. The candidate plan carries `counting_allowance_hours`, fixed BEFORE the counting rehearsal runs: 2 x the counting rehearsal's projected charge, in hours rounded up to 0.01 h. Projection (`partt_caps.counting_projection_seconds`, by the shared units x rates + processes x overhead): the sum over kinds of the subset units the measuring rehearsal ran x their measured rates, plus the sum over stages of the processes the counting rehearsal starts x the stage's measured per-process overhead, plus 2 x the training stage's overhead (the resumed worker of the forced pause and the stage process of the forced identity stop). If the measuring record carries no subset units (no dry-profile rate): processes the counting rehearsal starts (per stage, the stage process and one per task of the plan-derived subset) x the stage's overhead, plus the measured world-build seconds of each process, plus the same forced extra. The total cap = plan charge + counting allowance + 2 x projected registered stages. The freeze reads the counting ledger and refuses if the actual counting charge exceeds the allowance (an engineering stop: re-plan with `--supersede`, no cap edited by hand); otherwise the registered ledger starts from plan charge + the actual counting charge.
