After three sessions in the open condition (application and Claude Code open, the lead silent) ending PAUSED the next session runs in the CLOSED condition BY THE RULE: close the Claude application and Claude Code and launch it from a plain terminal (the same script); the change is recorded in `record.json` and goes into `docs/PARAMETER_CHANGELOG.md` (the lead's). ## Quiet addendum: what changes in cost, caps and the sequence (analytical unless marked)

The baseline's waiting is not charged either (ADR-0028 section 9).
- **Does the projection or any cap need recomputing because of the waiting? No** (analytical): the projection's timing figure is `sum(cpu seconds per update x (warm-up + repeats x updates))` from the measuring rehearsal's isolated timing (0.031 h in the lead's projection) and its throughput figure is the fixed 420 s from P10-S1; neither contains waiting, and the baseline is not in H_c.
